Mac version (free)

Download Karaoke Thai Keyboard for Mac (macOS 13 or later, Apple silicon and Intel). Unzip it and follow How to install.txt: drag the app into ~/Library/Input Methods, log out and back in, then add it under System Settings → Keyboard → Input Sources → Thai.

Mac demo, recorded in TextEdit (27 seconds).

Turn it on (iPhone and iPad)

  1. Open Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards.
  2. Tap Add New Keyboard… and choose Karaoke Thai.
  3. In any app, hold the 🌐 key and pick Karaoke Thai.
  4. Tap Karaoke Thai in the keyboard list and turn on Allow Full Access. iOS needs it for the free week, for the keyboard to see your unlock, and for a light tap on each key. The keyboard never goes online either way.

How to type

  • Space or Return types the first Thai guess.
  • Tap a guess to type that one. Swipe the bar sideways for more.
  • Tap the grey letters to keep them as English.
  • EN switches to plain English typing; ไทย switches back.
  • Words you pick come first next time.

Free plan and unlock

  • Free: 7 days with no limit (with Full Access on), then 20 Thai words a day, every day.
  • Unlock: one purchase in the app removes the limit on all your iPhones and iPads. No subscription, no ads.
  • Have a code? In the app, tap Redeem a code, or in the App Store tap your account picture → Redeem Gift Card or Code.
  • New phone? Tap Restore purchase in the app.
  • Bought it before it was free? It's already unlocked.

Common questions

I unlocked, but the keyboard still says the free words are used up. Turn on Allow Full Access for Karaoke Thai (Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Karaoke Thai), then open the app once.

The word I want isn't first. Many Thai words sound alike in English letters (khao = เขา, เข้า, ข้าว). Pick the one you want; the keyboard remembers. Typing more of the sentence also helps: kinkhao gives กินข้าว.

It doesn't work with my iPad's physical keyboard. iOS doesn't pass physical-keyboard typing to third-party keyboards. Use the on-screen keyboard.

The keyboard doesn't appear in password fields. iOS always uses its own keyboard there.
